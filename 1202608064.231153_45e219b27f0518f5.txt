to generalize this, we need to realize why    3 2 1 6 2 is so bad:


		it comes down to     realizing when something is the  "same computational state of the system"    


			and we can pin point exactly where these two points in the system     that are equivalent      are      in this OS:




				[3]   |   [2]   |   [1]   |   [6]   |   [2]   |  
				      ^         ^         ^         ^         ^
				      A         B         C         D         E



		so here, i have labeled 5 computational   temporal points in the system     labeled A, B, C, D, E 



					(where [X] denotes executing the instruction "X")







			here, we can see that    points     C and E    are actually equivalent: 



						at both   C   and  E 

	
							*0 has the value 1
							*1 has the value 0
							*2 has the value 0, etc...
	
							i has the value 1
	
							*n has the value 1 
	


					thus, us doing     the last two opertions in this sequence    6 and then 2  

									didnt actually accomplish anything!!!!!



								thus, is completely removable, and we would still have the exact same state of the system!!!!
